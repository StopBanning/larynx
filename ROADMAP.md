# Roadmap

The short version. The working plans, with the evidence for each finished task, are
[docs/PLAN-launch.md](docs/PLAN-launch.md) (getting Larynx to more people) and [docs/PLAN-v1.md](docs/PLAN-v1.md)
(the road to 1.0).

**Now**
- A signed download with automatic updates, so installing doesn't need Terminal.
- A landing page, and a measured comparison with the Mac's built-in dictation.
- Fewer leftovers: cut-off words ("defin- really") and "actually no" as a change of mind.

**Next**
- Names and look-alike words spelled from what is on your screen, read locally ("reel", not "real", when the word
  is in front of you). Built and running in test mode.
- Editors for your dictionary, vocabulary and snippets. Profiles per app.

**Later**
- Text that appears while you are still talking, for long dictations.
- Spoken punctuation and lists, writing styles, editing selected text by voice.
- The 24 other languages the speech model covers, measured before they are promised.
- Windows ([docs/WINDOWS.md](docs/WINDOWS.md)).

**Not planned:** accounts, a paid tier, cloud processing, telemetry.

Want something that isn't here? Open an issue. A missed word with the sentence that shows it is the most useful
thing you can send.
