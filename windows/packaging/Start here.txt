LARYNX FOR WINDOWS: START HERE
==============================

Larynx turns what you say into text, and it can record a meeting and write the notes for you.
Your voice stays on this computer. Only the meeting's written words go to the AI you choose for the notes.


1. OPEN IT
----------
Right-click the zip you downloaded, choose  Extract All , then open the folder it makes
and double-click  larynx.exe

Windows will warn you that the publisher is unknown (Larynx isn't signed yet).
Click  More info , then  Run anyway . This happens only the first time.

The first start downloads the speech model (about 670 MB). Leave it running until the
welcome window says it's ready.

Larynx then lives in the tray, the small icons by the clock. Its icon is a little
bar chart with a cursor. If you don't see it, click the  ^  arrow by the clock.

Right-click the Larynx icon and tick  Start Larynx with Windows , so it's there every
time the computer starts.


2. RECORD A MEETING
-------------------
Right-click the Larynx icon, then  Start recording this meeting .
A small window in the corner shows the time and a red dot. That's all you need to watch.

When the meeting ends, click  Stop . After a little while your notes open by themselves:
a summary, what was decided, who will do what, and everything that was said.

Using a second computer to record a meeting that's on your work computer?
That's fine. Put this computer near your work computer's speakers, turn the volume
up a little, and start the recording before the meeting starts. Larynx hears
everyone through the microphone.

Your meetings, any time: right-click the icon, then  Your meetings .


3. GET THE NOTES WRITTEN BY YOUR AI
-----------------------------------
Gemini, written automatically:
  Right-click the icon, then  Set Gemini key... . Get a free key at aistudio.google.com
  (sign in with Google, then Get API key), paste it in, and save. Every meeting's notes
  are then written by Gemini.

Copilot or Gemini, by hand:
  On any meeting's page, click  Copy for Copilot or Gemini , then click the Copilot or
  Gemini button next to it and paste (Ctrl+V) into the chat box.


4. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------
Right-click the icon, then  Send a report... . It saves a file. Send that file to whoever
set Larynx up for you. The report has no recordings and no words you said.

To quit Larynx: right-click the icon, then  Quit Larynx .
